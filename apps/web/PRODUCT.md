# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Paediatric haematology-oncology consultants and fellows at Belgian centres (BSPHO). They sit at a desktop between patients, preparing a family consult (confirm the diagnosis work-up, know the treatment flow, explain it) or checking the requirements before starting the next treatment step (blood counts recovered, response, re-stratification). They are expert, time-poor, and sceptical of anything that might misquote a protocol.

## Product Purpose
Protocol Packs turns each trial protocol PDF version into a navigable pack: diagnostics, treatment flow, requirements to start each step. Every clinical line is the protocol's own verbatim text, one click from its highlighted PDF page. Local hospital rules sit alongside, visibly distinct. Success: a clinician finds the exact line they need in seconds and trusts it because the source page is right there. No patient data anywhere.

## Brand Personality
Calm, exact, clinical. Reads like a well-typeset reference manual, not a SaaS dashboard. Confidence comes from showing the source, never from decoration.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards with hero metrics and card grids; EHR clutter; chatbot summaries that paraphrase; anything that hides the source page or version; consumer-health pastel friendliness.

## Design Principles
1. Verbatim or nothing: never paraphrase protocol text, always one click from the page it came from.
2. Speed to the line: search, deep links and section jumps beat browsing.
3. The decision is the headline: requirements to start, forks ("or") and re-stratification are the most prominent things on a step.
4. Provenance always visible: protocol, version and page are on screen and in print.
5. Local is not protocol: hospital rules look different at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Must be legible projected at the back of a room (large body text, strong contrast, no meaning by colour alone). Keyboard-first navigation (Ctrl+K / Ctrl+F palette).
